Is LUMA a government authority?

No. LUMA is an independent coordination service. We help organise your business registration and ongoing administrative work; the relevant public authorities issue official documents and determine filing outcomes.

What happens after my business is registered?

You receive the available official documents and a handover of the next steps. Managed compliance is a separate ongoing service for organising applicable returns, records and renewals.

Are official charges included in LUMA’s fee?

Your proposal separates LUMA’s coordination fee, applicable public charges and any third-party professional fees. Ask for that breakdown before agreeing to an engagement.
