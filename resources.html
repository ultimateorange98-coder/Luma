Choosing a structure: the first questions

Ownership, responsibilities and the practical questions to take into a professional discussion.

Begin with the people

Write down who will own and run the business. A solo operator and a group of founders may need different structures and governance arrangements.

Consider how the business will work

Your intended activity, funding plans, contracts and liability exposure deserve attention before choosing a legal form.

Confirm the legal route

Use the OHADA framework and the applicable Cameroon requirements with a qualified adviser. This guide does not determine the right form or promise a particular liability outcome.

Preparing for a registration discussion
LUMA field notes / Registration

Preparing for a registration discussion

A useful starting list of business information to organise before a dossier is agreed.

Describe the business

Prepare your intended activity, proposed name and operating location. Identify any sector-specific questions that need professional or official confirmation.

Identify the founders

Organise ownership intentions and discuss the identity, representation and signature requirements relevant to the route.

Check the official process

Cameroon’s Ministry of Finance identifies the CFCE as a point for business creation formalities. The exact documents and steps must be checked for your actual situation.

After registration: plan for continuity
LUMA field notes / Compliance

After registration: plan for continuity

The records, calendar and handovers to discuss once the business is formed.

Establish your actual obligations

A company’s responsibilities depend on its activity, legal form, tax position and employment situation. Ask the relevant advisers to identify what applies.

Give the calendar an owner

For each applicable item, agree who prepares the information, who approves it and who follows up. Do not rely on a generic website calendar as a filing determination.

Keep the evidence together

Organise the available submissions, receipts, confirmations and correspondence. Employer obligations should be checked with the CNPS where relevant.

Planning a Cameroon business from abroad

Questions about local coordination, representation and your practical next steps.

Map your availability

Explain where you are based, when you can participate and what you expect to do remotely. Avoid assuming that every formal step can be completed without local involvement.

Clarify representation

Confirm the identity, authority, signatures and local representation arrangements required for your circumstances with a qualified adviser.

Agree the handover

Set expectations for progress updates, approvals and the final documents. The official requirements should be checked before filing.

Reading an itemised service proposal
LUMA field notes / Pricing

Reading an itemised service proposal

Understand the separation between coordination, official charges and professional fees.

Start with the scope

An amount is meaningful only when the work is clear. Check the tasks, exclusions, dependencies and expected handovers.

Separate the fee layers

Identify the LUMA coordination fee, applicable public charges and any specialist professional fees. Ask how approvals, payments and receipts will be handled.

Understand changes

Discuss what happens if the work, official requirements or business circumstances change. A website estimate should not replace an agreed proposal.

General orientation, not a legal or tax determination. Confirm current official requirements and your circumstances with the appropriate professional.

A practical business document handover
LUMA field notes / Compliance

A practical business document handover

A simple framework for keeping administrative information useful.

Group documents by purpose

Separate formation documents, ongoing filing evidence, professional records and correspondence. A clear structure helps the next person find what they need.

Identify the owner

Agree who holds the official originals, who may access working copies and who updates the record. Keep sensitive information out of an initial website enquiry.

Record what is outstanding

A handover should distinguish available documents from pending work. Keep the next actions and responsible people visible.

General orientation, not a legal or tax determination. Confirm current official requirements and your circumstances with the appropriate professional.
