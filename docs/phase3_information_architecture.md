## 2. Page-by-Page Specifications

### Page 1 — Home (`index.html`)
- **Objective**: Establish institutional trust, articulate the value proposition, differentiate registration + managed compliance, and direct visitors to getting started or exploring the journey.
- **Key Sections**:
  1. *Institutional Header*: Logo, navigation with dropdown, active indicator, primary CTA.
  2. *Hero Section*: 
     - Badge: "Cameroon Business Formalization & Ongoing Compliance"
     - Headline: **"Build your business. We'll handle the paperwork."**
     - Subhead: "From initial registration at the CFCE to monthly tax filings and corporate compliance, LUMA coordinates the administrative work that keeps Cameroonian enterprises moving forward."
     - Dual CTA: "Get Started" (Primary) & "Discover How It Works" (Secondary).
  3. *Problem vs. Solution Matrix*: Real bottlenecks in Douala, Yaoundé, and across Cameroon (bureaucratic opacity, fragmented steps, missed tax deadlines, undocumented cash fees) vs. LUMA's coordinated single-point model.
  4. *Two Pillars*: Business Registration (Établissement & SARL) & Managed Compliance (Monthly Tax, Patente, CNPS, DSF).
  5. *Interactive Demonstration Widget*: Live interactive switcher showing the journey of a Cameroonian founder ("From registration to 12 months compliant operations").
  6. *Why LUMA Is Different*: Coordinated legal/accounting professionals, official TresorPay integration adherence, verifiable documentary proof.
  7. *Audience Categories*: First-time founders, growing SMEs, informal traders formalizing, and Diaspora founders launching ventures in Cameroon.
  8. *Key FAQs*: Quick accordion answering core questions.
  9. *Conversion Call to Action*: Clear, dignified closing section.
  10. *Editorial Footer*: Full sitemap, legal disclaimer (LUMA is an independent coordination platform, not a government body), verified contact info.

### Page 2 — How It Works (`how-it-works.html`)
- **Objective**: Demystify the 5-step lifecycle from first inquiry to ongoing corporate health.
- **Content**:
  - Step 1: Tell Us About Your Business (Structure diagnostic, activity, location).
  - Step 2: Understand Your Legal Route (OHADA structure comparison: EI vs. SARL vs. SARLU).
  - Step 3: We Coordinate Registration (Document preparation, CFCE submission, TresorPay handling, notary coordination).
  - Step 4: Receive Your Official Documents (RCCM, NIU / Carte de Contribuable, Attestation de Localisation).
  - Step 5: Stay Continuously Compliant (Tax calendar tracking, monthly DSF/TVA, CNPS declarations, annual patente renewal).
  - Interactive Step Visualizer with animated progress indicators.

### Page 3 — Business Registration (`business-registration.html`)
- **Objective**: Comprehensive guide to Cameroonian business structures and registration procedures without giving unauthorized legal advice.
- **Content**:
  - Detailed breakdown of structures:
    - *Établissement / Entreprise Individuelle (EI)*: Low complexity, single owner, no minimum capital, personal liability.
    - *SARL (Société à Responsabilité Limitée)*: Separate legal entity, protection of personal assets, required for multi-partner or capital operations.
    - *SARLU (SARL Unipersonnelle)*: Single-shareholder corporate protection under OHADA rules.
  - Required Documentation Checklist (ID/Passport, Criminal Record Extract/Declaration on honor, Lease agreement/Plan de localisation, etc.).
  - Realistic timeline guidance (CFCE targets ~72 hours upon complete file deposit, subject to official registry processing).
  - Interactive "Structure Recommender" diagnostic tool.

### Page 4 — Managed Compliance (`managed-compliance.html`)
- **Objective**: Showcase LUMA's primary differentiator: post-registration peace of mind.
- **Content**:
  - The "Post-Registration Reality": Business registration is day 1; tax and administrative penalties begin on day 15.
  - Core Compliance Pillars:
    1. *Tax Declarations (DGI)*: Monthly returns (TVA, précompte, acompte IS), annual DSF (*Déclaration Statistique et Fiscale*).
    2. *Patente & Municipal Contributions*: Annual business license verification and renewal.
    3. *Social Security (CNPS)*: Employee registrations, monthly tele-declarations, cotisations.
    4. *Corporate Governance*: Annual general meetings documentation, minutes, trade registry amendments.
  - Live "Interactive Compliance Calendar Preview": Visual interactive schedule of Cameroonian tax deadlines with notification alerts.
  - Documentary Evidence System: How LUMA archives official stamped receipts (*quittances*), tax clearance certificates (*ANR*), and compliance proofs.

### Page 5 — Pricing (`pricing.html`)
- **Objective**: Transparent, clear service pricing structure distinguishing LUMA service coordination fees from official government tariffs and third-party fees.
- **Content**:
  - Clear transparency disclaimer: Official government fees (e.g. CFCE/RCCM fees via TresorPay) and notary legal fees are paid directly or held in escrow without hidden markups.
  - Service Tiers:
    - *Registration Coordination*: Turnkey registration package (EI or SARL).
    - *Essential Compliance (Monthly / Annual)*: Dedicated compliance manager, monthly tax declaration coordination, deadline tracking.
    - *Comprehensive Enterprise*: Full corporate secretarial, multi-branch compliance, priority filings.
  - Interactive Cost Estimator / "Request a Tailored Quote" workflow.
