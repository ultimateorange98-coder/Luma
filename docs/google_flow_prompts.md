# LUMA — Google Flow Production Prompt Pack

This prompt pack provides production-ready prompts designed for **Google Flow** (video and image generation). Every prompt is formulated to produce authentic, cinematic, non-stereotypical visual assets that harmonize with LUMA’s official navy identity (`#021D48`), warm ivory tones, and professional Cameroonian business context.

---

## Asset A — Cameroon Business Environment (Hero Video / Banner)

- **Intended Placement**: Homepage Hero Background & "How It Works" Header (`index.html`, `how-it-works.html`)
- **Type**: Subtle Ambient Video Loop / Cinematic Still Fallback
- **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` (1920×1080 or 4K)
- **Duration**: 8 to 12 seconds seamless loop
- **Visual Style**: Cinematic editorial realism, 35mm film aesthetic, rich natural depth of field, subtle motion blur, high dynamic range.
- **Environment**: A bright, contemporary, architecturally refined creative studio / technology office overlooking the vibrant urban skyline of Douala or Yaoundé (soft green tropical foliage and modern architecture visible through large glass windows).
- **Lighting**: Soft morning golden-hour natural window light diffused by sheer curtains, gentle rim lighting, warm highlights on dark wood desk.
- **Camera Movement**: Slow, elegant motorized slider push-in at eye level, subtle parallax between foreground desk elements and background window.
- **Google Flow Prompt**:
  > *"Cinematic documentary shot of a confident, contemporary Cameroonian woman entrepreneur in her early thirties sitting at a minimalist solid mahogany desk in a bright, modern office in Douala. She is thoughtfully reviewing clean, printed corporate reports and glancing towards a sleek laptop. Her attire is elegant modern business-casual in deep navy and warm cream tones. Gentle natural morning sunlight pours through floor-to-ceiling windows showing soft-focus urban tropical trees and architectural lines in the background. Calm, focused, dignified atmosphere. Shot on 35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, f/2.0, natural authentic skin tones, subtle dust motes in soft sunbeams, no exaggerated movements, peaceful corporate focus."*
- **Negative Prompt**:
  > *"text, typography, watermarks, fake logos, exaggerated luxury, sports cars, jewelry, poverty tropes, mud huts, traditional tribal costumes in tech office, posed stock-photo smiles, pointing at screens, distorted hands, extra fingers, cartoonish, 3D render look, purple AI lighting, cyan neon glow, overly saturated colors."*
- **Still-Image Fallback Prompt**:

## Technical Export Guidelines for Web Implementation
1. **Video Codec**: H.264 / MP4 (CRF 22, 1080p, no audio track) and WebM for optimal browser performance.
2. **Video File Size Target**: Sub-3MB for background loops; use `preload="metadata"` and `playsinline autoplay muted loop`.
3. **Still Image Format**: WebP (quality 85) with JPEG fallback; explicit `width` and `height` attributes to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
4. **Poster Frame**: Always capture frame 001 of each video as a WebP poster to ensure instant visual rendering before video stream initializes.
