Reviewed 7 November 2026. Evidence: refreshed Home, How It Works, Pricing, Resources, FAQ, Contact and About desktop/mobile captures; the unchanged Registration and Compliance compositions from round 2; current shared source; live homepage story checks; the completed `audit.json` read during this pass.

## Verdict

This round makes the page differences substantive. How It Works now has a panoramic five-station journey, Resources and FAQ introduce search in their first screen, Pricing gives the planes a semantic fee key, and Contact brings its first mobile fields forward. The signature story now fits both final objects on a phone, keeps one canvas, uses a continuous exit and offers meaningful static selections with reduced motion.

The result is coherent, distinctive and usable within the authorized local-form scope. It is close to the proposed exceptional band, but this review does not yet support “all nine pages at least 9.0.” The main remaining visible defect is About's image aperture, plus a consistent small-type/material-detail ceiling. The scores below remain tied to execution rather than the desired number.

## What earned the improvement

- **Philosophy:** the entire set now consistently uses folded structural forms, navy/ivory/mineral surfaces, large geometric display type, ruled information rows and restrained state motion. The panoramic journey and search-first utilities create variety without changing the visual rules.
- **Hierarchy:** the mobile Home silhouette is immediately visible; utility search is now part of the opening; Contact shows its first two fields in the 844px capture; Pricing explains what its image stands for. The main action and supporting information are easy to distinguish.
- **Detail:** earlier clipping, joined words, rod geometry, harsh cast shadows and blank folio face are resolved. Remaining small controls, tightly keyed fee labels and the About aperture prevent the exceptional band.
- **Functionality:** the current `audit.json` reports 45 layout checks with no failures, 18 accessibility check entries with no recorded violations, and overall pass. The parent's focused interaction checks cover the stated local-form and navigation scope. The independent motion recheck below confirms the prior story defects are corrected. Automated success supports this axis only; it does not raise art-direction scores.
- **Innovation:** Home's recognizable brand form opens into the two services through actual facet motion and a controlled camera change. The same renderer is reused, the static fallback remains meaningful, and the new journey composition connects five stations to actual stage links. Quieter pages appropriately prioritize useful content; their innovation score reflects that their visual/interaction details remain familiar, not that they need arbitrary spectacle.

## Remaining critical or high-priority defects

No unresolved P0 defect was found in this review's inspected scope. One P1 visual defect should be resolved before final delivery:

**About image aperture:** the refreshed `about-1440.png` and `about-390.png` show the photo only as a small triangular sliver at the bottom of the L-shaped opening; the rest appears navy/blank. This reads as a misaligned image plane or occlusion artifact, not a composed human image. Fit/reposition the photo behind the entire opening for the actual camera and pointer range, or use a deliberate clean void instead. Do not claim a successful image-framing concept from the current capture.

## Three highest-return polish changes

1. **Finish About's signature composition.** Resolve the aperture fill first, then ensure the arch has a clean silhouette, consistent edge highlights and a meaningful image crop at desktop and phone sizes. The concept is specific to LUMA; the current partial texture is the most visually expensive remaining error.

2. **Give small semantic labels proper UI scale.** Pricing's fee key and several mobile pause/caption labels are around 8–10px. Increase semantic fee labels and usable controls to a comfortable scale, keep the key aligned to its actual plane, and place it beneath the art if the phone layout cannot fit it beside the planes. Decorative captions can remain secondary, but controls should look intentional and remain easy to operate. The five-stage panorama also becomes tiny on phones; its large stage links are useful, so reduce excessive empty art space or offer a closer selected station rather than expecting viewers to read tiny document lines.

3. **Add selective material and content specificity.** Refine the three main object families, rather than adding more effects: a convincing fold joint for the emblem, slight separation/binding detail for folios, and a less icon-like continuity mechanism. Keep thickness, edge lights and scale consistent. Pair them with a few concrete service sentences, replacing redundant “considered / clarity / ambition / next chapter” phrases where they add no information. This is the remaining difference between an attractive procedural system and a finished bespoke identity. It should be a focused craft pass, not another structural rewrite.

## Page-specific notes

- **Home:** the most complete expression. Preserve its three-line mobile headline, first-screen L, readable real-HTML service story and restrained cinematic section. Further detail work should strengthen the emblem's physical fold without changing the established composition.
- **How It Works:** the wide station arrangement is a real departure from the service-page split. Its stage navigation makes it useful. The phone panorama is small but no longer blocks understanding because the links remain readable; make the relationship between station and selected stage more explicit if polishing further.
- **Registration:** comparison, diagnostic and document imagery form a practical sequence. Its result can still be more closely tied to a nearby visual than to the offscreen hero. This is a refinement, not a functional blocker.
- **Compliance:** the illustrative calendar is clear and responsibly labeled. Its clock remains a literal familiar icon. A more specific record/coordination detail could raise innovation without increasing motion density.
- **Pricing:** the semantic color key is a substantial improvement. Final key type size and alignment are more valuable than another animation. Keep quote-based pricing until actual fees exist.
- **About:** the arch concept provides page identity, but the image-fill defect is unresolved in the inspected capture.
- **Resources:** the search-first layout and typographic covers work well together. The book sculpture is now a secondary cue, which suits the page. Keep direct article access and search feedback.
- **FAQ:** the search-first opening and visible first question on mobile are markedly more useful. Its small folded-sheet cue can remain quiet; more WebGL would not improve the job.
- **Contact:** the first two phone fields are now present in the opening capture, and the copy clearly explains local preparation. The absence of an external destination is explicitly deferred by the user and is not a blocker.
