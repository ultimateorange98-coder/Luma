## Evidence for the exceptional-band judgments

**Philosophy:** the family is now unusually consistent through its complete page set: L-derived geometry, satin navy/ivory materials, ruled editorial structure, two type roles, quiet utility motion and differentiated page tasks. The archive, dossier, arch, pricing layers and panoramic route share an identity without repeating one hero template. The higher consistency score reflects the visible material/copy details, not a new test result.

**Hierarchy:** Home identifies both service and primary action while showing a real brand object on phones. Resources/FAQ now place search above the fold rather than behind a marketing prelude. Pricing explains its planes. Contact prioritizes the real form; its first fields are visible on mobile. Registration's two-line “Your business. Made official.” and Compliance's “Your filings. Kept in view.” are more concrete and easier to scan than the earlier abstract phrases.

**Detail:** the fee labels are readable at the phone layout's intended size, the legend has a distinct row, and visible controls measured 11px type with 44px height where displayed. The L seal and navy binding make the registration folio specific to the brand; archive spine rules and calendar marks are coherent additions. About's coplanar L-shaped image now fills the aperture without a rectangular backing or bottom sliver. Contact is the exception described below.

**Functionality:** the current scoped experience has the previously recorded passing layout, accessibility and interaction checks, and this direct-live nine-route pass observed no JavaScript page errors. The form is intentionally local prepare/download; no backend is required by the current user instruction. Physical-device performance, real-user timing and exhaustive assistive-technology coverage remain outside the available verification and are not implied by this score.

**Innovation:** Home's branded fold story, About's image aperture and How It Works' spatial five-stage arrangement are earned by their content. Resources/FAQ appropriately use a branded utility-first treatment rather than escalating motion. Registration and Compliance remain lower on this axis because their main user actions produce conventional nearby text changes while any 3D response happens in a hero the visitor has scrolled past.

## Three contained fixes for the remaining pages

### 1. Contact — resolve fan intersections (P1)

`r4-live-contact-1440.png` shows rear-sheet binding and face strokes crossing the front face: broken navy strips and doubled pale lines appear in the center. This is a physical arrangement/depth problem introduced by the new fan, not useful extra detail. Increase sheet separation, change the fan angles/pivots or simplify to a physically separated open folio. Ensure each sheet's own details stay attached to its face and do not cut through its neighbor at the settled pose or pointer extremes.

The form itself remains accepted. No external destination, new consent flow or backend is requested as part of this fix. Once the fan is clean, its detail axis can be reassessed from the actual view.

### 2. Registration — give the diagnostic an in-place visual result

Its comparison, wording, document imagery and new branded folio are strong. The remaining innovation gap is that the ownership/result selection primarily swaps a heading and explanatory copy; the scene response is offscreen. A compact HTML/SVG ownership-to-dossier diagram beside the result would explain the chosen route immediately. For example, one founder vs multiple founders can feed a visibly grouped dossier, with the existing careful explanatory copy. Keep it a conversation aid, not a legal decision engine. This can be lightweight and static with reduced motion.

### 3. Compliance — connect a selected task to information and evidence

The monthly/annual schedule is clear and the hero now reads as recurring filings. Give one selected preparation item an adjacent visible handover view: records needed → coordinated filing → evidence retained. Use the already illustrative scope; do not imply live filing status, legal deadlines or actual customer records. This makes the page's distinctive “kept in view” promise tangible where the visitor interacts, rather than only rotating a clock in the offscreen hero.

Neither tool needs a full new WebGL renderer. Small HTML/SVG state changes can complete the content relationship while retaining the existing runtime budget.
