## 2. Anti-AI-Slop Design Directives (Adopted from OpenDesign)

To ensure LUMA looks like a **\$150k+ bespoke digital agency production** rather than an AI-generated template, the following strict anti-patterns from `open-design` are enforced:

1. **NO Generic 3-Card Symmetrical Grids**:
   - *AI Slop Pattern*: 3 identical cards side-by-side with a round icon and generic paragraph.
   - *OpenDesign Solution*: Asymmetrical magazine layouts, 7:5 split heroes, full-bleed media banners with floating glass metadata tags, and alternating editorial row treatments.
2. **NO Synthetic CSS Placeholders Where Media Belongs**:
   - *AI Slop Pattern*: CSS linear-gradient boxes pretending to be software or photography.
   - *OpenDesign Solution*: Real generated high-definition cinematic video loops (`.mp4`) and authentic editorial photography (`.jpg`) from `C:\Users\Elma kuo\Documents\Luma generations`.
3. **NO Edge-to-Edge Sticky Flat Navbars**:
   - *AI Slop Pattern*: Full-width rectangular bar with harsh drop shadow glued to top edge.
   - *OpenDesign Solution*: Elevated floating pill navigation with backdrop-filter blur, hairline metallic border, and subtle badge indicators.
4. **NO Hollow Tech Buzzwords**:
   - *AI Slop Pattern*: "Revolutionizing business in Cameroon with seamless AI-powered compliance."
   - *OpenDesign Solution*: "Coordinating CFCE incorporation dossiers, notarized statutory deeds, monthly DGI teledeclarations before the 15th, and annual DSF filings."
