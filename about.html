Your information

A considered
first enquiry.

The current form

The enquiry form processes your entries in your browser and prepares a text file for you to download. It does not send them to LUMA, store them in a website database or create a service engagement. No identity documents or confidential tax information are needed for this first brief.

When contact is connected

The owner will supply the submission destination and the relevant privacy information before live collection begins. A future engagement should set out the actual scope, responsibilities, terms and handling of information.

External references

Resource links lead to independent official websites. Their own information and privacy practices apply when you visit them.
