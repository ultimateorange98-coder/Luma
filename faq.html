10 answers to explore.

Is LUMA a government authority?

No. LUMA is an independent coordination service. We help organise your business registration and ongoing administrative work; the relevant public authorities issue official documents and determine filing outcomes.

What happens after my business is registered?

You receive the available official documents and a handover of the next steps. Managed compliance is a separate ongoing service for organising applicable returns, records and renewals.

Which business structure should I choose?

That depends on your ownership, activity and liability needs. The registration page includes a starting-point diagnostic. A qualified adviser should confirm the structure and current requirements before you commit.

Can I start a business from abroad?

You can begin planning and preparing your information remotely. Any representation, identity checks, signatures and local presence requirements depend on your circumstances and must be confirmed before filing.

How long does registration take?

Timing depends on a complete dossier, your chosen structure, professional preparation and the relevant authorities. Your itemised proposal should explain the expected stages and dependencies; the site does not promise an official approval date.

Does every company have the same filing calendar?

No. Obligations depend on your legal form, tax position, employees, activity and other factors. The calendar shown on this site is an explanatory example, not a determination of your filing deadlines.

Are official charges included in LUMA’s fee?

Your proposal separates LUMA’s coordination fee, applicable public charges and any third-party professional fees. Ask for that breakdown before agreeing to an engagement.

Do you guarantee registration or tax clearance?

No. Public authorities control official decisions. LUMA’s role is to coordinate preparation, follow-up and documentation within the agreed service scope.

Does this website send my enquiry?

The current form prepares a brief that you can download. It does not send your information or create an engagement. A submission destination will be connected when the owner supplies the contact details.

What should I prepare for a first conversation?

A short description of your activity, the number of founders, your intended location and whether you need registration or ongoing support. Please do not include identity documents, passwords or confidential tax records in an initial enquiry.
